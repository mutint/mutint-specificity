mutint-specificity asks whether genome evolution was specific to the treatment each population evolved under: whether samples from one treatment were mutated in more of the same genes than samples from different treatments, which genes are concentrated in one treatment, and whether some treatments accumulated more mutations or different kinds. Its page is Specificity, in the sidebar's experiment section. The statistics are evospec's; please cite Deatherage, D.E., Kepner, J.L., Bennett, A.F., Lenski, R.E., and Barrick, J.E. (2017) Specificity of genome evolution in experimental populations of Escherichia coli evolved at different temperatures. PNAS 114:E1904–E1912, doi:10.1073/pnas.1616132114.
