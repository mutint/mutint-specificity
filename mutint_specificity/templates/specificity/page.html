Specificity

Was evolution specific to the treatment each population evolved under? This compares the genes mutated in samples from different treatments, as in Deatherage et al. 2017. Each sample with a treatment is one genome. A mutation counts towards a gene when breseq's annotation names exactly one gene it inactivates, overlaps or sits in the promoter of (within 150 bp of the start).
